Accessibility at Arcade

Our goal is WCAG 2.2 Level AA. We are actively improving accessibility and do not yet claim conformance.

Available features

Keyboard-operable controls, optional color-vision-friendly and monochrome palettes, enhanced contrast, larger controls and text, reduced motion, and alternatives to card dragging. Many games include descriptive labels and live status messages. Settings are stored on this device.

Known limitations

Full screen reader gameplay, focus during changing game states, target size and contrast across all boards, and zoom and reflow require further verification. Automated scans only cover some accessibility requirements.

Feedback

Report barriers through the Arcade issue tracker. Please include the game, device, browser, and assistive technology if relevant. Do not include private information.
